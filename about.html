01Our story

Same curiosity.
New orbit.

For years, the University of Lethbridge Physics Club was a small, devoted group of majors who met to argue about thought experiments, watch science-fiction films and take telescopes out on clear nights.

As more astronomy and mathematics students found their way to those meetings, the name stopped fitting the people in the room. In 2025 the members voted to become the Physics, Astronomy & Mathematics Association — keeping everything that worked, and opening the door wider.

Today PAMA is a non-profit, student-run club. We organise talks, observing nights, study support, workshops and socials, and we try to make the physical and mathematical sciences welcoming to everyone — from the first-year who isn't sure about their major to the graduate student finishing a thesis.

02What we do

To equip students with the skills, resources and community to thrive as future scientists — and to share the wonder of it with everyone else.

01

Talks & colloquia

Faculty, graduate students and guest speakers on the questions at the edge of what we know — pitched for curious undergraduates.

02

Observing nights

Club telescopes, dark coulee skies and experienced observers to show you where to look. Beginners especially welcome.

03

Study sessions

Peer-led help for first- and second-year physics, astronomy and math courses, from upper-years who remember exactly where it gets hard.

04

Workshops

Practical skills the curriculum skims: Python, LaTeX, data analysis, scientific writing and presenting your work.

05

Research & careers

Panels, lab tours and advice on summer research, graduate school and the many careers a quantitative degree opens up.

06

Community

Film nights, socials, competitions and outreach — because the best ideas usually start over pizza.

03Values

What we
hold constant.

Four principles guide how we run events, make decisions and treat each other.

∂

Curiosity

Every question is a good question. We'd rather explore a half-formed idea together than pretend we already understand.

±

Honesty

Good science shows its error bars. We're open about what we know, what we don't, and how club decisions and funds are handled.

∪

Inclusion

The union of everyone. Our events are open to all students, programs, backgrounds and levels of experience.

Σ

Community

The sum is greater than its parts. We look after each other — academically, and otherwise.

06Code of conduct

A safe place
to be curious.

Everyone at a PAMA event — members, guests, speakers and executives — agrees to a few simple things.

  1. Be respectful. Critique ideas, never people. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.
  2. Make room. Invite quieter voices in, and remember that every expert was once a beginner.
  3. Look out for each other. Especially at night-time observing sessions and off-campus trips.
  4. Speak up. If something feels wrong, tell any executive member — in person or by message — and we will act on it confidentially.
